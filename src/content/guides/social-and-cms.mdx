# Social & CMS

Makinari agents can publish content directly to your social media profiles and content management systems.

## When to use

- You want the Copywriter agent to generate and publish a weekly blog post.
- You want the Growth agent to post regular updates to LinkedIn or Twitter.

## Connecting Social Networks

Makinari uses **Outstand** to manage social media authentication and publishing.

1. Go to **Settings > Channels > Social** in the dashboard.
2. Click **Connect Account**.
3. Select the platform (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook Pages, Instagram Business).
4. Authorize Makinari to publish on your behalf.

Once connected, you can create a Requirement like: *"Write a technical post about our new API and publish it to LinkedIn."* The agents will handle the drafting, image generation, and publishing via the Outstand integration.

## Connecting a CMS (Webflow)

Makinari natively supports publishing to Webflow.

1. Go to **Settings > Channels > CMS**.
2. Select Webflow and provide your Webflow API Key.
3. Select the target Site and Collection (e.g., "Blog Posts").
4. Map the Makinari content fields (Title, Body, Image) to your Webflow Collection schema.

When the Copywriter agent finishes a blog post, it will be sent directly to your Webflow CMS as a draft or published post, depending on your Requirement instructions.
