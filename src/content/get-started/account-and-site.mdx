# Account and Site

Before you can use any of Makinari's features, you need an account and a Site. A **Site** is the top-level workspace where your agents, leads, campaigns, and API keys live.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to the [Makinari Dashboard](https://app.makinari.com).
2. Sign up using your email and password.
3. Verify your email address to log in.

## 2. Create a Site

When you log in for the first time, you will be prompted to create a Site.
1. Click **Create Site**.
2. Enter your company or project name.
3. Configure your default timezone and language.

## 3. Choose your path

With your Site created, choose how you want to use Makinari:

- Are you setting up agents to handle incoming visitors? → **[Inbound Path](/get-started/inbound)**
- Are you launching outbound sales campaigns? → **[Outbound Path](/get-started/outbound)**
- Do you want agents to work autonomously on goals? → **[AI Goals](/get-started/ai-goals)**
- Are you integrating Makinari into your app backend? → **[First API Request](/get-started/first-api-request)**
- Are you building an AI agent locally in Cursor? → **[MCP in 5 Minutes](/get-started/mcp-in-5-minutes)**
