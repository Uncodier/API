# Path: AI Goals

The AI Goals path introduces you to **Requirements**: high-level objectives that you assign to your Makinari agents. Instead of building step-by-step automations, you tell the agents what you want, and they figure out how to achieve it.

**Goal:** Define your brand context and create your first Requirement.

## Prerequisites
- A Makinari account and Site.

## Step 1: Set up Brand Context

Agents need to understand your business to represent you properly.

1. In the [Dashboard](https://app.makinari.com), go to **Settings > Context**.
2. Fill out the core business details:
   - What does your company do?
   - Who is your target audience?
   - What is your brand tone (professional, friendly, technical)?
3. Save the context. All agents across your Site will now use this knowledge base.

## Step 2: Create a Requirement

A Requirement is an AI Goal with a specific objective and a token budget.

1. Navigate to **Requirements** in the dashboard.
2. Click **New Requirement**.
3. Select a category (e.g., "Content Generation", "Lead Research", "System Automation").
4. Write clear instructions in the "Brain" section. For example:
   > *"Write a 1000-word blog post about the benefits of AI in B2B sales. Use our brand tone and target VP of Sales. Publish it when done."*
5. Allocate a token budget (e.g., 50,000 tokens) to cap the agent's spending.
6. Click **Activate**.

## Step 3: Watch the agents work

Once activated, the CMO agent analyzes the Requirement and delegates tasks.
1. Click on the active Requirement.
2. Under the **Commands** tab, you will see the specialized agents (like the Copywriter or Data Analyst) picking up tasks.
3. You can review the **Artifacts** (like the draft blog post) as they are generated.
