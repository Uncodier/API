# Get Started

Welcome to Makinari. The way you get started depends entirely on your goals and technical background. 

Before proceeding, ensure you have created an [Account and a Site](/get-started/account-and-site). Then, choose the path that best matches your objective:

## 1. Business Operators

If you want to use the dashboard to set up AI agents and automate your business processes without writing code:

- **[Path: Inbound](/get-started/inbound)** — Set up a tracking script and website chat widget to capture incoming visitors.
- **[Path: Outbound](/get-started/outbound)** — Import leads and launch your first AI-driven outbound campaign.
- **[Path: AI Goals](/get-started/ai-goals)** — Give your agents brand context and assign them high-level Requirements to execute autonomously.

## 2. Developers & Integrators

If you want to integrate Makinari's backend capabilities into your own application, or connect your local AI IDE:

- **[Path: First API Request](/get-started/first-api-request)** — Learn our dual-authentication model, generate an API key, and make a server-to-server request.
- **[Path: MCP in 5 Minutes](/get-started/mcp-in-5-minutes)** — Connect your Cursor IDE or Claude Desktop to the Makinari MCP server to give your local AI access to your project.
